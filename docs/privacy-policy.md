# Atelier Halle Media Manager — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 4, 2026_

Atelier Halle Media Manager ("the app") is a private tool used by Atelier
Halle to prepare and schedule posts to its own Instagram and Pinterest
accounts. It is not offered to the public, and it is used only by its owner
with accounts the owner controls. This policy covers the app only; it is
published at https://atelierhalle.com/privacy.

## What the app accesses

When an account is connected, the app uses the official Pinterest and
Instagram APIs to access only what it needs to publish:

- Basic account details: account ID, username, display name, profile
  picture link and account type, to confirm the right account is connected.
- Pinterest boards, so a board can be chosen for each Pin.
- Permission to create Pins and Instagram posts on the connected account.

The app does not read followers, messages, analytics or other people's data,
and it does not collect Pinterest or Instagram passwords. Access is granted on
Pinterest's and Meta's own sites: through Pinterest sign-in, and through
Meta's business tools for Instagram.

## How data is used

Data from Pinterest and Instagram is used only to publish the owner's own
posts to the owner's own accounts and to show their status in the app. Every
Pin and post is created individually by the owner. Data is not used for
advertising, profiling, analytics, or training machine-learning models.

## Where data is stored

- Everything stays on the owner's own computer. There is no app server,
  account system or cloud database.
- Access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain and are never written to
  ordinary files or logs.
- Photos and captions are stored locally and sent only to the platform they
  are being published to.
- For Instagram only, each photo is placed briefly in private Cloudflare R2
  storage so Instagram can fetch it, then deleted. It is reachable only
  through a link that expires within 15 minutes.

## Retention

- Access tokens are kept until the account is disconnected.
- Account details and the chosen board for each Pin are kept locally with
  the account's record until the owner deletes the app's data.
- For published posts, the app keeps the post's ID and link as a local
  posting history until the owner deletes it.

## Sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties. The only services involved are
Pinterest and Instagram themselves, and Cloudflare R2 for the short-lived
Instagram uploads described above. The app contains no analytics or tracking.

## Deleting data

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens immediately.
Access can also be revoked at any time from the connected-apps section of
the Pinterest or Instagram account settings.

Deleting the app's local data folder removes everything else it stored.
